Using unblank

What does unblank do?

unblank helps you practice everyday conversations. Each day, Pip, your coach, texts you one short scenario. An AI character plays the other person and you reply in your own words. When the scene ends, you guess how you came across, and Pip gives you one thing that worked and one thing to try next time. Most scenarios take two or three minutes.

How does the daily text work?

During setup you choose when you’d like to practice: morning, day or evening. Around that time each day, Pip sends a notification with the day’s scenario. Tap it to start. You can also open the scenario from the Today screen, even if notifications are off.

The daily scenario is free. The texts are scheduled on your phone; practicing needs an internet connection.

How do I change the time of my daily text?

Tap your avatar on the Today screen to open Profile, then change your practice time in the Practice section. Your upcoming texts move to the new time.

To stop the texts, turn off notifications for unblank in iOS Settings → Notifications.

How do I turn off streaks?

Open Profile and turn off Streaks in the Practice section. You’ll still see which days you practiced, but there’s no streak count and no streak reminders. You can turn streaks back on at any time.

Your subscription

How do I cancel my subscription?

Apple manages unblank Pro subscriptions. On your iPhone:

  1. Open Settings and tap your name (your Apple ID) at the top.
  2. Tap Subscriptions, then unblank Pro.
  3. Tap Cancel Subscription.

Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal or free-trial end date to avoid the next charge. You keep Pro until the end of the period you’ve paid for. Deleting the app doesn’t cancel a subscription.

Apple handles all refunds. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I restore my purchase?

If you subscribed but unblank isn’t showing Pro, for example after reinstalling or on a new iPhone, open Profile, go to Subscription and tap Restore. Make sure you’re signed in to the App Store with the Apple ID you subscribed with.

Still not working? Write to us and we’ll help.

Privacy and safety

How do I delete my data?

In the app, open Profile → Privacy:

  • Delete history clears your past conversations and their feedback.
  • Delete my data erases your account and everything in it, immediately. It can’t be undone.

Deleting the app from your phone doesn’t delete your data from our server, and deleting your data doesn’t cancel a subscription. If you no longer have the app, write to us. The details are in our privacy policy.

Is unblank therapy?

No. unblank is practice, not therapy. If anxiety is getting in the way of work, school or leaving the house, a therapist (CBT) is the best-evidenced help.

What if I’m in crisis?

Please don’t wait for a reply here. If you’re thinking about hurting yourself, or you’re in crisis:

  • In the US, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline).
  • In the US, text HOME to 741741 (Crisis Text Line).
  • Anywhere else, find a line at findahelpline.com.

If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.

unblank isn’t a crisis service, and messages here aren’t read around the clock. Our safety protocol explains what the app does in these moments.
