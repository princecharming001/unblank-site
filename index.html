    A text a day.

    Every day there’s one conversation to have, open until midnight. Pip, your coach, texts you around the time you pick: your manager in the elevator, someone new at a party, the barista who knows your order.

  2. Jordan · AI character

    Tuesday morning. Your manager steps into the elevator with you. Six floors to go.

    Your one jobAsk one follow-up question.

    Oh, hey. Busy day?

    Pretty full. Good to have you back, though. How was the week off?

    Honestly? Great. I ran every morning. Now it’s back to that coffee machine.

    02

    Practice with an AI character.

    An AI character plays the other person, with a name, a mood and a day of their own. You reply in your own words. There’s no script: finding the words is the practice.

  3. Pip

    You guessed “not great.” It read better than that.

    Win

    “How was the week off?”

    You noticed something real and asked about it. That’s what got Jordan talking.

    Next

    Jordan handed you two hooks: the running and the coffee machine. Pick one up.

    Try: “Every morning? How early?”

    03

    One thing that worked, one thing to try.

    Guess how it went. Pip quotes one line that worked and gives you one move for next time. Then you see your score out of 10 across four skills. Every conversation earns XP toward your next level.
